# Treely Yours Launch

A TikTok Shop brand taken from idea to live storefront in days.

## The launch

Treely Yours was co-founded with Adam Abdelouahad in November 2026. The brand went from idea to live storefront in days, with five personalized ornament products listed, a brand website, and an open affiliate program from launch week.

## The storefront

- Five personalized ornament products listed on TikTok Shop.
- A brand website with a product personalizer that hands off to TikTok Shop checkout.
- An open affiliate program at 15 percent commission, with creators locking in from launch week.

## How it runs

The whole operation runs through the AI agent system I designed and direct: content, outreach, supply, tracking, support, and finance.

This repo is a case study in architecture and documentation only. It contains no production code, prompts, supplier costs, margins, or sales figures.
